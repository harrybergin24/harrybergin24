# Projects

### *[Volatility Trading](https://github.com/harrybergin24/Trading-Volatility)*

Within this project there are three subprojects based around looking at volatility as something to take a position on, all coming at volatility as a concept from different angles. The first sub-project of back testing variance swaps is an attempt to generate an insight into how variance swaps have returns and what are the ways to boost a non-directional volatility trading strategy. Secondly, the structuring aspect is looking at pricing vol products, I am initially starting with pricing variance swaps and forward starting swaps in the future I hope to extend this further after I have learnt about new derivatives on vol such as vol options. Finally, I am working on creating a predictive volatility model using machine learning, I am taking Kaggle's Machine Learning and Feature Engineering courses as well as other resources to prepare me to take on this project.


This project will once completed contain 3 parts:
- [Variance Swap Back Test](<https://github.com/harrybergin24/Trading-Volatility/blob/28018e7d068859b50d0c2b028ff5b5e8fd106b8f/Variance_swap_trading/FinalVS/VS_TRADING_README.md>)
   
- [Structuring volatility products](<https://github.com/harrybergin24/Derivatives-Dashboard/blob/main/Vol_derivatives/vol_products_dashboard.md>)
  
- Forecasting Volatility

### *[Derivatives Dashboard](https://github.com/harrybergin24/Derivatives-Dashboard/blob/0a0dc89f01629d63590f9ace8da371376ece5368/README.md)*
